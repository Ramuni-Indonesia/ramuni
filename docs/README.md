# RAMUNI Marketing Website and Blog Pack

Folder `docs/` sekarang dikhususkan untuk dokumen marketing website, money-site, blog, content, SEO, social launch, dan final brand implementation. Dokumen product-development tetap menjadi referensi requirement agar klaim marketing sesuai kemampuan produk.

## Urutan dokumen handover

1. [05-marketing-blog-plan.md](./05-marketing-blog-plan.md) — sitemap, funnel, blog, CMS, SEO, analytics, dan conversion plan.
2. [10-marketing-pages-blog-content-brief.md](./10-marketing-pages-blog-content-brief.md) — brief architecture, navigation, section, content, resources, help center, blog, dan landing page.
3. [08-marketing-website-blog-developer-handover.md](./08-marketing-website-blog-developer-handover.md) — handover teknis website marketing, blog, SEO, analytics, consent, tracking, ads-readiness, dan QA.
4. [13-final-brand-implementation-brief.md](./13-final-brand-implementation-brief.md) — kontrak implementasi logo final Lipat Arah, warna, Plus Jakarta Sans, favicon, CMS theme, website, blog, dan campaign creative.
5. [instagram-3x3-launch-brief.md](./instagram-3x3-launch-brief.md) — production brief social launch Instagram 3×3 yang sudah memakai brand final Lipat Arah, lengkap dengan copy, visual direction, asset mapping, crop, scheduling, moderation, dan reporting.
