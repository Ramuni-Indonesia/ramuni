# Homepage visual sources

## Product evidence

The workspace screenshots used by the homepage are captured from the RAMUNI SaaS
environment with controlled demo data. The homepage uses the full WebP captures
under `public/website-original/product-screens/saas-e2e/full/` and keeps separate
desktop, tablet, and mobile files so the page does not stretch one low-resolution
image across every viewport.

## Industry photography

These local WebP files are sourced from Unsplash pages and are stored locally so
the homepage does not hotlink remote image URLs:

- `fnb-counter.webp` — [A counter in a coffee shop](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-counter-in-a-coffee-shop-with-coffee-cups-on-it-Up4NVezcxK8), Syahril Fadillah.
- `retail-shelves.webp` — [Small store interior with shelves of goods](https://unsplash.com/photos/small-store-interior-with-shelves-of-goods-Q6lJLndyw-4), Bernd Dittrich.
- `community-shop.webp` — [People relaxing outside a small shop](https://unsplash.com/photos/people-relaxing-outside-a-small-shop-with-goods-on-display-1IWT7khkL4I), David Kristianto.

The credit is rendered next to each image in the homepage card. The files are
WebP derivatives created for the site; the source pages remain the attribution
reference.

## Mockup direction

The homepage product-proof section intentionally uses direct product cards rather
than a device frame. Device mockups can be introduced on a specific product page
when the screenshot is readable at that viewport; they should not hide the full
workspace sidebar or crop the evidence that the page is trying to explain.
