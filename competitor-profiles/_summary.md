## Strategic gap

RAMUNI does not need more section types. It needs fewer, better differentiated page grammars:

- Product pages prove a capability.
- Solution pages dramatize one operational problem and its resolution.
- Industry pages show a day-in-the-life workflow.
- Role pages show permissions, exceptions, and handoffs.
- Feature pages disclose availability and limitations.
- Pricing helps someone choose, without making unapproved capabilities look available.

The strongest opportunity is to combine Majoo-level concreteness, Mekari-level trust placement, and HashMicro-level demonstration with RAMUNI's more credible “evidence before action” positioning.
