## Positioning and page grammar

Majoo positions itself as a practical POS and business-management ecosystem for Indonesian entrepreneurs. Its strongest copy uses recognizable daily tasks, business types, support expectations, social proof, pricing, and trial.

## Strengths relevant to RAMUNI

- Concrete UMKM vocabulary instead of abstract product language.
- Business-type fit is prominent.
- Pricing and trial routes are easy to find.
- Social proof makes the product feel established.

## Weaknesses / cautions

- Several pages are extremely long.
- Pricing and supporting blocks repeat.
- The product breadth produces dense catalogs.

## Implication for RAMUNI

Use concrete daily-work scenarios per industry and role, while keeping each route to one argument and five or six chapters.
