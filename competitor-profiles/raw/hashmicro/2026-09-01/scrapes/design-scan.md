# HashMicro — raw design scan

- Captured: 2026-09-01
- Method: direct HTML fetch from public pages; headings and structural counts only.
- Homepage: `https://www.hashmicro.com/id/`
- ERP page: `https://www.hashmicro.com/id/sistem-erp`
- Retail page: `https://www.hashmicro.com/id/hash-retail-software`

Observed headings:

- Homepage H1: “Software ERP Berbasis AI untuk Kerja Lebih Cerdas”.
- Homepage moves from AI-native differentiators to business functions, industries, AI assistant, enterprise proof, FAQ, content, and a final CTA.
- ERP page: AI personas/product concept → outcomes → functions → industry fit → live/product demo → trust → FAQ.
- Retail page: retail outcome → AI use cases → POS/inventory → cross-team system → product demo → trust → FAQ.
- The fetched ERP and retail HTML contained 45–46 video tags, so its cinematic approach should not be copied wholesale.

Design implication: strong role/industry specificity and early demonstration create energy, but the page length and media density are a warning for RAMUNI.
