Observed headings:

- Homepage H1: “Aplikasi Manajemen Bisnis, Operasional Serba Otomatis”.
- Homepage foregrounds operational savings, business-type fit, ecosystem, social proof (“45.000 wirausaha di 600 kota”), pricing, and trial.
- Product hub uses task-specific statements: transactions, performance monitoring, promotions, business-specific products, add-ons, offer, support, and trial.
- F&B page uses concrete contexts such as orders, payments, busy hours, menu changes, debt/receivables, and multi-outlet reporting.
- Pricing and F&B pages are very long and repeat several blocks; they are useful for copy specificity, not for page length.

Design implication: Majoo speaks in daily UMKM language and makes business-type fit obvious. RAMUNI should borrow that concreteness while keeping a shorter editorial structure.
