## Positioning and page grammar

Mekari positions itself as an integrated Indonesian business-software ecosystem. Its public hubs are comparatively concise: identify the category, show product or solution paths, establish scale/trust, and close with one sales action. Its industry and UMKM pages become longer, but keep trust near the top.

## Strengths relevant to RAMUNI

- Recognizable trust metrics and customer proof appear early.
- Product, solution, division, scale, and industry paths are clearly separated.
- Hub pages do less explaining than SEO detail pages.
- The ecosystem story makes cross-product value easy to understand.

## Weaknesses / cautions

- Detail pages can become long product catalogs.
- Several headlines use broad “growth/digital transformation” language.
- Large navigation and product breadth would be excessive for RAMUNI's current scope.

## Implication for RAMUNI

Use shorter hubs, one visible product-family model, and honest trust/status proof. Do not copy the breadth or generic growth language.
