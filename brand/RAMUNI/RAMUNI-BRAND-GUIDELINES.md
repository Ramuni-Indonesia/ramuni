# RAMUNI Brand Guidelines

Final: Lipat Arah - 25 July 2026

## Brand idea

RAMUNI means Meramu Insight untuk Menavigasi Bisnis Indonesia. The folded ribbon composes raw business signals into one practical direction. Its inner angle offers a subtle second reading of RA without becoming a literal monogram.

## Logo system

- RAMU uses Ink Navy; NI uses Turmeric Yellow.
- Reverse versions are all white.
- Clear space: at least half the mark height.
- Minimum digital size: 24 px preferred; 16 px only for browser tabs.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink Navy | #0B3045 | Trust, wordmark, mono logo |
| Ramu Teal | #168C8C | Fold, navigation, growth |
| Turmeric Yellow | #F2B134 | NI accent, niat, Indonesian warmth |
| Warm Rice | #F4F0E7 | Human background |
| White | #FFFFFF | Reverse logo |

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans: ExtraBold for display, Bold for headings, SemiBold for labels, Regular for body. Fallback: Inter, Arial, sans-serif.

## Voice

Pintar tanpa menggurui, modern tanpa terasa asing, profesional namun dekat dengan UMKM. Lead with the business meaning and end with a useful action.

## Do not

No gradients, sparkles, robots, AI nodes, literal arrows, extra letters inside the mark, shadows, rotation, stretching, or recoloring NI.
